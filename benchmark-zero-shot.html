Benchmark overview

The zero-shot benchmark evaluates methods that operate without benchmark-specific labeled training data, making it the most constrained public evaluation setting in ACCIDENT.

  • Tasks: temporal localization, spatial localization, and collision type classification.
  • Official score: unified score, with per-task metrics shown separately.
  • Evaluation note: the current local preview uses all labeled clips because no dedicated zero-shot split column exists in labels.csv.
