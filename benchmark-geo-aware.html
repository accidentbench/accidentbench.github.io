Benchmark overview

The out-of-distribution benchmark tests whether methods transfer across regions, making it the main public setting for studying geographic shift in surveillance accident detection.

  • Tasks: temporal localization, spatial localization, and collision type classification.
  • Official score: unified score, together with the three task metrics.
  • Focus: robustness when test regions differ from those seen during training.
